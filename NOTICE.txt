Fogline - an Age of Empires IV replay reader. Free, non-commercial; for personal use.

Age of Empires IV (c) Microsoft Corporation. Fogline was created under Microsoft's "Game Content Usage Rules"
using assets from Age of Empires IV, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with Microsoft.

This package contains no game files, models, textures or icons. tools/setup.py reads the needed values and
models from your own installation of the game, on your PC; icons are fetched from data.aoe4world.com when a page is
built with --icons. Do not share the generated models/, icons/, data/game_weapons.json or the pages made from them
outside the Game Content Usage Rules.

Third-party data and tools
- Unit / building / technology tables in data/ are derived from aoe4world data (github.com/aoe4world/data,
  data.aoe4world.com), used under its terms (non-commercial, with the Microsoft notice above).
- AOEMods.Essence (github.com/aoemods/AOEMods.Essence, MIT licence) reads the game's archives; tools/setup.py downloads
  it from its GitHub releases. The .NET runtime it needs is installed from Microsoft (dot.net) when missing.
- The 3D page loads three.js (MIT licence) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
