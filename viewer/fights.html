Win odds before: Lanchester square law from the armies near the fight at its start (damage per second against the other side's mix, with bonus damage and armour, times current HP; base stats, no upgrades, range or micro). Upset = the side given worse odds lost less value.
Target choice: value destroyed per second by each attack (damage × target cost ÷ target HP), compared with the best target within the attacker's reach at that moment; 1 = always the best available. Attack lines: ≥ 0.8, 0.5–0.8, < 0.5.
Focus fire: 1 = all attackers on one target, 0 = every attacker on a different target.
Overkill: share of the damage on dying units that was not needed to finish them. Needs the 4×/s fight samples; with 1/s data it is rough.
Damage dealt: HP each unit lost, split among the enemy units recorded attacking it (by their expected damage against it), grouped by attacker type. Unattributed = HP lost with no recorded attacker (arrows in flight, splash, attackers outside the sampled area).
